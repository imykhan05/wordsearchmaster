Delete your Word Search Master account

You can permanently delete your account and all data associated with it at any time. This page explains what gets deleted and how to request it.

Option 1 — from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Word Search Master and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — by email, if you can't access the app

Email mohammaddeveloper38400@gmail.com from the address associated with your account (or include your in-app display name / uid if you know it) with the subject line Delete my account. We will process the request within 30 days and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in record
  • Your level progress, stars, coins and streak history
  • Your achievements
  • Your entries on every leaderboard (global, per-language, weekly, daily)
  • Your friends list, on both sides of every friendship

What this does not delete

Game progress stored only on your own device is not touched by an account deletion request — that data never left your device in the first place. To remove it too, uninstall the app or clear its storage from your phone's Settings.
