# happens it becomes a screenshot and a one-star review, so the generator

# Format: one word per line. Blank lines and lines starting with # are
# ignored. Entries are normalized with ScriptNormalizer before matching, so
# case does not matter here.

# are aggressive: "ASS" also blocks GLASS and CLASS wherever they appear as a
# run of cells. That is the intended trade — a slightly duller grid beats an
